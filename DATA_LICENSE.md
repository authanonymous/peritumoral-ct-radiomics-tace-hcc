# Data licence and attribution

The tables in `data/` and `results/` are derived from the public WAW-TACE dataset (Zenodo record 12741586) and are shared under the same licence, **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**, which permits re-distribution and re-use of the licensed work provided the creators are appropriately credited.

`data/external/clinical_data_wawtace_v2_15_07_2024.csv` is an unchanged copy of the public WAW-TACE clinical table.

**Intended use.** The WAW-TACE Zenodo record additionally states that the dataset "can be used for scientific research purposes only" and that access "is not contingent upon the requirement for co-authorship or the establishment of a research collaboration." This repository, and the tables and clinical data redistributed in it, are shared strictly for that purpose — scientific research and reproducibility of the accompanying peer-reviewed manuscript — consistent with both the CC BY 4.0 licence and the dataset creators' stated intent. Re-users of this repository should likewise restrict their use of the WAW-TACE-derived content to scientific research.

Please cite the source dataset:
Bartnik K, Bartczak T, Krzyzinski M, et al. WAW-TACE: A Hepatocellular Carcinoma Multiphase CT Dataset with Segmentations, Radiomics Features, and Clinical Data. Radiol Artif Intell 2024;6(6):e240296. Dataset: https://zenodo.org/records/12741586 (CC BY 4.0).

No images or segmentation masks are included. The data contain no direct identifiers.
